# Third-party and reused code

* `engine/` is the git submodule [sdl-2d-game-engine](https://github.com/andrealaforgia/sdl-2d-game-engine) (MIT, (c) 2026 Andrea Laforgia), the same
  engine `super-fabio-bro` is built on. It is a pinned dependency and is not modified here. The game's `rng_t` and `aabb_collision` come from it.
* `scripts/check_boundary.py` and `scripts/boundary_stubs/` are adapted from `super-fabio-bro` (same author).
* SDL2 and SDL2_image (zlib licence) are used by the engine and linked dynamically; they are not vendored.
* No third-party art, audio, fonts or sprite sheets are used. All graphics are generated by `tools/assetgen/*` and all audio by `tools/audiogen/*`.
